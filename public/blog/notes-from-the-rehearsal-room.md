# Notes From the Rehearsal Room

A rehearsal room is never only a place to work. It is also a culture, a system of attention, and a rehearsal of trust.

## Three observations

- The clearest ideas are often found in the pauses.
- Silence can be a collaborative partner when it is invited intentionally.
- A production grows when the room is allowed to be honest about what it does not yet know.

The most useful notes are rarely the loudest ones. They are the ones that help everyone move closer to the real story.
