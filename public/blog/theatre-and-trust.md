# Theatre and Trust

Trust is the quiet engine of a production. A creative team cannot do its deepest work if the room feels like a performance of certainty rather than a shared act of discovery.

## A producer's responsibility

- Protect the vision without protecting it from revision.
- Make room for artists to say what is true, not just what is efficient.
- Build partnerships that reward generosity and accountability.

A show becomes durable when everyone senses that the work is being held, not merely managed. That is how risk becomes something brave rather than reckless.
