# Building Rooms That Breathe

The best theatre rooms hold air as much as they hold sound. A producer learns quickly that the design of a space can shape the distinct emotional weather of a production before the first line is spoken.

## Why atmosphere matters

- A room should offer clarity without feeling rigid.
- Seating, sightlines, and pacing are all part of the story.
- The audience should feel invited into the work, not managed by it.

> The goal is not to impress the room, but to make people feel remembered inside it.

When a stage can flex between intimacy and scale, the work feels alive. That generosity is what allows a text to breathe.
